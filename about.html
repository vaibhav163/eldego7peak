About Eldeco Group

The Eldeco Group has been a leading name in real estate development across North India since 1985, led by Eldeco Infrastructure and Properties Ltd. As published on the official Eldeco Group website, the group has delivered more than 175 projects spanning townships, high-rise condominiums, industrial estates, malls and office complexes, with 30+ projects in various stages of active execution and more than 30,000 satisfied customers.

Eldeco's presence spans Lucknow, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Delhi and other North Indian cities. This website covers only Eldeco's projects in Noida, Greater Noida and along the Yamuna Expressway.

Design & Credibility

Eldeco ensures unique and innovative designs for all its projects and avoids repetition in external design, so each completed project becomes a landmark in its local geography. The group follows strong processes, a company-wide ERP system and a robust MIS, with a high emphasis on transparency and conformance to statutory norms.
