Eldeco Group has launched three residential projects in Sector 22D on the Yamuna Expressway, Greater Noida — Eldeco Echoes Of Eden (RERA: UPRERAPRJ125342/02/2026), Eldeco Whispers Of Wonder (RERA: UPRERAPRJ752382/09/2025) and Eldeco Ballads Of Bliss (RERA: UPRERAPRJ921261/05/2025). All three offer 3 BR air-conditioned homes in a fast-growing corridor shaped by the upcoming Noida International Airport.

Sector 22D sits 0.5 km from Jaypee Sports City and about 3 km from the Buddha International Circuit, with Gaurs International School (5.3 km), Galgotias University (5.9 km) and Kailash Hospital (4.5 km) in the neighbourhood. Ballads Of Bliss lists the Noida International Airport at 32.5 km. Compare the three projects below and enquire on WhatsApp for prices, floor plans and site visits.
